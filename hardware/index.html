WHY CRUCIBLE

A proven foundation, deployed locally.

Crucible is an open-source cyber-simulation framework developed by Carnegie Mellon University's Software Engineering Institute. Its Caster component supports infrastructure-as-code deployment to Proxmox VE, allowing environments to be versioned, cloned, reset, and expanded instead of rebuilt by hand.

BEFORE YOU MOVE IT

Does your equipment fit?

Send the exact model and configuration first. We would rather decline equipment responsibly than accept hardware we cannot use.

Please send

  • Manufacturer and exact model
  • CPU model and quantity
  • Installed memory and DIMM details
  • Storage controller and installed drives
  • NIC models and port speeds
  • Power supplies, rails, caddies, and available licenses
  • Known faults, drive health, and firmware status

Not part of this request

  • Miscellaneous desktops and laptops
  • Monitors, printers, and general office equipment
  • Consumer drives offered as active range storage
  • Equipment that depends on unavailable subscriptions or licenses
  • Hardware without enough information to verify compatibility
